Our approach

  • We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.
  • We write in plain language, give images text descriptions and keep text readable when it is enlarged.
  • We check new pages and features for accessibility as the website changes.

Report a problem
